Common Questions

Everything you need to know before enrolling

Do I need prior experience?

Not for the Starter track. We start from absolute zero. If you have experience, we tailor the pace accordingly.

How are sessions delivered?

Live on Google Meet or Zoom. Sessions are recorded so you can rewatch them anytime.

What if I miss a session?

Reschedule for free — just let me know 24 hours in advance. Recordings are also available.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, on completion of each track you get a certificate you can share on LinkedIn.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. 50% upfront and 50% halfway through is the most common plan. Just ask.

Is there a refund policy?

Full refund within the first 2 sessions if you're not satisfied. After that, no refunds.
