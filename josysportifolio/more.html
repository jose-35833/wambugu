About Me

I am a Full Stack Developer, UI/UX Designer, and Trainer with 3+ years of experience building digital products that people actually use. I work with clients worldwide from my base in Kenya — helping startups, small businesses, and individuals ship great websites and mobile apps.

My work spans the full product lifecycle: research, design, development, and deployment. I believe great products come from understanding users first, then engineering clean, scalable solutions that feel effortless to use.

What I Do

I split my time between building for clients and teaching others to do the same. Both roles keep me sharp — the real-world problems I solve on client projects become the case studies I teach in my courses.

As a developer: responsive websites, full-stack web apps, cross-platform mobile apps, and APIs.
As a designer: user research, wireframing, prototyping, design systems, and UI implementation.
As a trainer: personalized 1-on-1 courses in web, UI/UX, and app development, plus project mentorship.

My Approach

Every project — whether a client website or a student's first HTML page — starts with the same question: what problem are we actually solving? I focus on clarity over cleverness, results over process, and shipping over endless planning.

I value clean code, honest communication, and on-time delivery. If something isn't working, you'll hear it early — not after the deadline.
